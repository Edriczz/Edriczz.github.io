# EY DS – AI and Data Team

We look forward to you joining us in the EYDS AI and Data team.

- Bachelor's Degree or above in Information Systems, Computer Science, Informatics, Electronics and Communications, or related disciplines.
- Minimum 1–2 years (for Associate level) and 3–5 years (for Senior level) in data engineering.

- Collect, process, and analyse large data sets to identify trends and patterns.
- Develop and maintain dashboards and reports using Microsoft Power BI.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data needs and provide actionable insights.
- Conduct data quality assessments and ensure data integrity.
- Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders in a clear and concise manner.

## Technical Skills

- Identifying trends and patterns in data
- Data visualization and dashboard development
- Data quality assessment and ensuring data integrity

## Tools

- SQL-based databases
